## What happened

Reviewed case 5 in `dev_docs/ggai-introduction-report.qmd` after the side-by-side was flagged as unconvincing. The problem was substantive: paper #1 Figure 3 is a layered comparison matrix, while the report compared it against an unrelated single-panel GO enrichment dotplot pulled from case 4B.

Replaced that weak comparison with a new report-only, reproducible figure generated by `dev_docs/scripts/build-case-tf-enrichment-matrix.R`. The new asset keeps the same visual grammar as the paper figure: shared TF-gene x-axis, upper time-point strip, family annotation band, and lower enrichment dot matrix. The prose now calls it a structural reproduction rather than implying biological data equivalence.

## Findings / decisions

- Sharing `dot size + color` encodings is not enough to claim a figure is comparable when the chart problem itself differs.
- The truthful claim here is "same figure grammar, toy data," not "same result" or "same semantics."
- The report prompt example also needed to move up one abstraction level: from "make a dotplot" to "build a layered comparison matrix with shared x-axis and annotations."
